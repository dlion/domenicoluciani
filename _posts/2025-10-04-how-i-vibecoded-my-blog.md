After a few months of inactivity, I thought about renovating my blog, changing categories, style, add functionalities and a personal touch. I wanted to have something new and the same time I didn't want to spend hours searching for the right theme to modify, dive deeper into new techs and spent lots of times just to fix some css. So, I decided to vibecode my new blog theme using ChatGPT 5 and its brand new coding agent: Codex 🤖


## Old times

I still remember when I wanted to change my blog's theme, I needed to go through endless websites looking for a theme that was similar to what I wanted to have as a base, and then spending weeks or months to adapt it 

## Why Codex?

Why not? I have a personal ChatGPT plus subscription and [Codex](https://chatgpt.com/features/codex) is included with it, so I decided to give it a try. I know, there are other (maybe?) better agents out there but I didn't want to spend so much time digging what was the best one for this job, I just used what I have had available. Simple as that.

## From where did I start?

I work every day with ChatGPT doing [agumented coding](https://tidyfirst.substack.com/p/augmented-coding-beyond-the-vibes) and I know enough about the context, and how it would improve the LLM's replies and efficacy if done in the right way with the right instructions, but this time I decided to give just a small initial context and see how far it would go before starting breaking stuff. And I can say that it went far from what I was initially expecting.
